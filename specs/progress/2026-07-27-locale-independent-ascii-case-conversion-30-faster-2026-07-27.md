- ✅ **Locale-independent ASCII case conversion, ~30× faster (2026-07-27).** `march_string_to_lowercase`/`to_uppercase` called ctype.h's `tolower`/`toupper` per byte. Correctness: those are **locale-sensitive**, and March strings are UTF-8 — measured on macOS, `en_US.ISO8859-1` maps `0xC3` → `0xE3`, and `0xC3` leads every 2-byte UTF-8 sequence, so conversion could silently corrupt the encoding. March never calls `setlocale`, but any linked library or embedding app can, which makes string semantics hostage to global process state. Replaced with a branch-free unsigned-wrap ASCII mapping: identical output under C, en_US.UTF-8, en_US.ISO8859-1 and tr_TR.UTF-8, and **0.60s → 0.02s** on `bench/string_case`. Correctness checked exhaustively rather than sampled — an independent driver compares the new mapping against C-locale ctype for all 256 byte values including the `'A'-1`/`'Z'+1` boundaries. The 30× was verified not to be work disappearing: `allocs` (406) and `copy_bytes` (~401MB) are unchanged. **`bench/string_case`'s checksum cannot detect that failure mode** — it sums `byte_size`, invariant under case conversion — now noted in `specs/benchmarks.md`.
