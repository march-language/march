Parser rule `lambda_body: lambda_stmts expr` added to `lib/parser/parser.mly`; `lambda_stmts` greedily consumes leading `let`/`linear let` bindings before the final expression. Works for all lambda forms (`fn x`, `fn (a,b)`, `fn ()`, `fn _`). No token-filter changes needed — NL suppression outside match contexts is already correct. Zero new parser conflicts. 5 new parse tests; `syntax_reference.md` and `CLAUDE.md` updated.
