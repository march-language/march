- ✅ **String performance phase 1: the measurement apparatus (2026-07-27).** Built to decide March's string representation question with data instead of asymptotic reasoning. `MARCH_STRING_STATS=1` counters in `runtime/march_runtime.c` (allocation count/bytes, size histogram at 7/15/23/31/63/255, bytes copied, frees, peak live bytes, non-string `march_alloc` counts), hooked into `march_string_alloc` and both `march_decrc` free paths, off by default behind a lazy env check shaped like `gc_trace_on`, verified at −0.34% overhead when off. Six benchmarks in `bench/` each isolating one cost, documented in `specs/benchmarks.md`, plus `bench/run_string_bench.sh` with an asserted-checksum gate (proved to fire), per-platform RSS normalization, and a `--verify-overhead` mode. Verdicts in `specs/2026-07-26-string-performance-profile.md` against criteria fixed before measuring: **SSO indicated** (91.7% of 24M allocations ≤23 bytes; doubling allocations doubles wall time), **array-returning split indicated** (1.82× slower than slice-walk while copying 1.43× the bytes — the difference is 9,000,120 cons cells vs 0), **views NOT indicated** (copy volume 0.55–0.79× of input, not the ≥2× required; each field is copied exactly once), SIMD search worth doing at ~0.5 GB/s but not the top win, **phase 3 gate triggered** (parallel scan plateaus at 3.97× on 8 of 10 performance cores, unexplained by cores or bandwidth). Three implementation corrections the measurements forced: an `obj_allocs` counter (cons cells are not strings, so the split/slice pair could not attribute list cost without it), byte-loop copy accounting for `to_lowercase`/`to_uppercase`/`reverse` (they transform while copying and never call `memcpy`, so `string_case` reported 1MB copied for 400MB of work), and O(n) reformulations of two benchmarks whose planned `index_of`-then-slice-the-tail shape was O(n²) (`String.index_of` has no start-offset variant).
