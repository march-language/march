- ✅ **JIT codegen: bitwise builtin fix** — `int_and/int_or/int_xor/int_not/int_shl/int_shr/int_popcount` now work in JIT. Root causes: (1) missing from `builtin_names` in `defun.ml` caused erroneous free-variable capture in closures; (2) no inline `EApp` case in `llvm_emit.ml`. Fixed both: added to `builtin_names`, `is_builtin_fn`, `builtin_ret_ty`, and added `EApp` inline cases emitting LLVM `and/or/xor/shl/ashr/ctpop` intrinsic. Also added `llvm.ctpop.i64` to the preamble and a parity test suite for bitwise builtins.
