- ✅ **Cap(IO.Spawn) — task-spawning capability** — `IO.Spawn` added as a new leaf under `IO` in `io_cap_hierarchy`. Five builtins mapped in `builtin_cap_table`: `task_spawn`, `task_spawn_link`, `task_spawn_steal`, `task_spawn_with_cancel`, `get_work_pool`. The body-scan pass (Phase 2) automatically enforces the new cap: modules that spawn tasks without `needs IO.Spawn` get a warning; `needs IO` (or any ancestor) satisfies it. 3 new tests in `cap_body_enforce` group: missing spawn warns, declared spawn suppresses warning, parent `IO` covers spawn.
