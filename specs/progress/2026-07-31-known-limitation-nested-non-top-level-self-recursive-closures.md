- [ ] **Known limitation: nested (non-top-level) self-recursive closures never get TCO** — `fn go(...) do ... end` closures compile as an indirect `$apply` call, never a loop back-edge, regardless of the fixes above. Only top-level `pfn`/`fn` self-recursion is eligible for TCO. Not blocking (stdlib call sites were rewritten to top-level `pfn` helpers instead), but worth a compiler warning if a future change reintroduces the pattern in a hot path.
