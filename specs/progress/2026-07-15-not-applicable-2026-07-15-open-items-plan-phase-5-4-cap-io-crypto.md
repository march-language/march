- [x] ✅ **NOT APPLICABLE (2026-07-15, open-items plan Phase 5.4) — Cap(IO.Crypto) does not apply: crypto is PURE March, not IO.** `stdlib/crypto.march` implements `sha256`/`sha512`/`hmac` etc. entirely in-language ("intentionally simple to verify without external libraries") — there are NO `crypto_*` builtins/externs on the trunk. Hashing/HMAC is pure computation (deterministic, no side effect), so gating it behind an `IO.*` capability would be semantically wrong — an IO cap is for observable side effects, which pure crypto has none of. Closed as won't-implement. (If a future crypto op is genuinely effectful — e.g. a hardware-RNG-backed key gen — it belongs under `IO.Random`, not a new `IO.Crypto` leaf.) Spec: `specs/capability-system-design.md §Future Capabilities §6`.
