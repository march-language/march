Any program containing a record literal or update failed `test/test_fmt.exe`'s "record literal" round-trip check ("formatted source does not parse"). Root-caused via `superpowers:systematic-debugging`: reproduced with `test_fmt.exe -e`, traced the parse failure to `parser.mly`'s single `record_field_expr` production, then found the mismatched `=` in `format.ml`. Fixed both `ERecord` and `ERecordUpdate` field-printing to emit `:` instead of `=`. `test/test_fmt.exe` now 24/24 (was 23/24); full quick suite (`scripts/run-tests.sh -q`) green, 779 tests, no regressions.
