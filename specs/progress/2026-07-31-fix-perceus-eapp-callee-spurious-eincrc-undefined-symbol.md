- ✅ **Fix Perceus EApp callee spurious EIncRC (`@__` undefined symbol)** — `lib/tir/perceus.ml`: commit `831e315` changed `needs_rc(TFn _)` to `true`, causing Perceus to insert `EIncRC` for the callee name in every `EApp`. After defun, `EApp` callees are always top-level function symbols (code addresses), never heap-allocated closures. For operators like `&&` and `||`, whose `llvm_name` maps to `@__`, this produced a call to an undefined symbol that failed to link. Fix: removed the callee `f.v_name` from (1) liveness propagation in `live_before`, (2) `find_inc_vars` in `insert_rc_expr`, and (3) live-before propagation in the EApp arm — three targeted changes covering all call-paths. `ECallPtr` (indirect dispatch through closure structs) is unaffected. All 147 tests pass.
