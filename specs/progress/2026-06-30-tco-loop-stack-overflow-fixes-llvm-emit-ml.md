- ✅ **TCO loop stack-overflow fixes (llvm_emit.ml)** — (1) `llvm.stacksave`/`llvm.stackrestore` around every TCO loop body, fixing unbounded per-iteration stack growth from `alloca`s inside the loop; (2) a second Perceus tail-call shape (`ESeq(EApp(self,args), dec_chain)`, arising from wildcarded constructor fields like `Cons(_, t)`) that was silently compiled as non-tail recursion. `Bytes.from_string`/`Bytes.length` now handle 200,000-element inputs; previously crashed above ~10,000.
