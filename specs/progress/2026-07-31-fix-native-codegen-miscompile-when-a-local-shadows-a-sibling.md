- ✅ **Fix native-codegen miscompile when a local shadows a sibling module-level fn** — `rename_tir_vars` (lib/tir/lower.ml) qualified every `AVar` matching a sibling module fn name without checking whether the name was locally bound, so a param/pattern/let binding like `fields` in `pfn blank_fold(fields, acc)` next to `fn fields(schema)` was rewritten to the qualified global and emitted as the fn's address (SIGBUS) or a zero-arg `"gl"` call ("use of undefined value" at clang time). The rename walk now threads an in-scope binder set (fn params, ELet/ELetRec bindings, ECase branch vars) and skips shadowed names. Regression fixture `test/imports/param_shadow_native/` compiles natively and runs. Found via depot's `Depot.Schema.blank_fold`.
