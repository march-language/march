## What exists now

- **The findings** (design section 5.3), each over two `SNAPSHOT` envelopes a
  window apart, in two languages with the same ids and thresholds:
  `forge/lib/diagnose.ml` (OCaml, for forge) and `stdlib/diagnose.march`
  (`Diagnose.findings(before, after)`, and `Diagnose.run(c, window_ms)` for
  a program to check itself).

  | Finding | Fires when | Severity |
  |---|---|---|
  | `mailbox.growth` | an actor's waiting work (mbox + held) rose across the window, to at least 10 | critical if one actor holds over half of a node queue of 100+ |
  | `mailbox.over_limit` | an actor at its limit under drop_new / drop_old, and the node's dropped count rose | warning |
  | `sched.saturated` | a scheduler over 95% busy with work in the run queue | warning |
  | `sched.idle_imbalance` | one scheduler over 80% while another is under 20% | warning |
  | `crash.loop` | a supervisor's children crashed 3+ times | critical in the last minute, warning in the last hour |
  | `rc.climb` | live heap objects up over 10% with the actor count within 5% | warning |
  | `epoch.stuck` | an epoch is draining | warning |
  | `epoch.old_units` | units pinned two or more epochs behind current | warning |

  Utilisation is compared by integer cross-multiplication on both sides
  (`100 * idle < 5 * wall` for "over 95%"), never through a rounded
  percentage or a float, so the two implementations cannot disagree at a
  boundary.
- **Shared fixtures**, `forge/test/fixtures/diagnose/`: eleven before/after
  pairs shaped exactly like the socket's replies (generated by `gen.py`), and
  `expected.txt`, one line per fixture naming its findings. Two must stay
  silent: `healthy`, and `rc_climb_with_actors` (the heap grew with new
  actors).
- **`forge diagnose [--socket P | --env E] [--window ms] [--dump FILE] [--json]`**:
  the `march.diagnose/1` envelope per target, with `coverage` listing every
  probe that ran, those that cannot (`cluster.suspect`: no cluster section
  until R1.3; `names.lost`: global-registry Lost events are not recorded),
  and the partial one (`mailbox.over_limit` uses the node's dropped total).
  Exit 0 nothing, 1 warnings, 2 critical, 3 unreachable (the worst over all
  targets). `--dump` writes the raw pair in the fixture format.
- **`forge top [--socket | --env] [--sort attr] [--n N] [--window ms] [--once]`**:
  an ANSI redraw of the busiest actors with a one-line node header; rate
  sorts (`slices`, `msgs_in`, `msgs_out`) rank the change over the window,
  which is also their refresh; others refresh every max(1 s, 4 x the
  snapshot's cost). `--once` prints one frame without screen control.
- **`forge status [--socket | --env] [--json]`**: with a `topology.toml`,
  `forge topology status`'s report (`Reconcile.status_nodes`, newly
  factored out of `status_text`) followed by one observe line per node
  (actors, queued, RSS, lifetime busy %, crashes in the last hour, deepest
  mailbox); otherwise the `[hot-reload]` hosts'.
- **Remote sends count**: `observe_count_send` (stdlib-only builtin, both
  backends) bumps the calling actor's `msgs_out`. Called where every remote
  send passes: `NodeSend.cast_schema` (raw and typed sends) and
  `Node.enqueue_tagged(_schema)` (the queue's writer goes straight to the
  socket).

## Tests

- `forge/test/test_diagnose.ml`: every fixture yields exactly its
  `expected.txt` ids; exit codes; the envelope and its coverage.
- `test/native/diagnose_fixtures.march`, compiled and interpreted: the March
  findings over the same fixtures print `expected.txt` exactly (dune diffs
  against that file), and `Diagnose.run` runs on the program itself.
- Red checks: raising March's `rc.climb` threshold alone fails the March
  rule (`rc_climb` loses its finding); changing OCaml's 95% check alone fails
  `test_diagnose` (`sched_saturated`).
- `test/native/diagnose_runaway.march` with the real binaries: a node whose
  mailbox grows under a producer; `forge diagnose` exits 2 with a critical
  `mailbox.growth`, `forge top --once` names the actor, `forge status --json`
  reports it as the deepest mailbox, and a missing socket exits 3.
- `forge/test/test_observe_cli.ml`: the summary, the top frame and the status
  JSON over fixture snapshots.
- `test/native/node_send_loopback.march`: the reply an actor sends with
  `NodeSend.cast` shows in its `msgs_out` (0 with the count removed).

## Deviations from the plan

1. **`crash.loop` from the crash ring, not supervisor config.** The plan's
   "restarts within one of max" needs each supervisor's `max_restarts`, which
   the snapshot does not carry; the ring's per-supervisor counts in the last
   minute / hour say the same thing without it.
2. **`mailbox.over_limit` uses the node's dropped count**: per-actor dropped
   counts do not exist (reported in `coverage.partial`).
3. **`cluster.suspect` and `names.lost` are reported unavailable** until the
   cluster section (R1.3) and global-registry events exist.
4. **Finding rows in March are integer lists** (pids, scheduler ids,
   supervisor pids or epochs); forge's OCaml rows are full objects. The
   shared test compares ids and severities.
5. **Remote sends are counted in `NodeSend`**, not only `Node.send`: the
   plan's site missed raw `NodeSend.cast` replies.
