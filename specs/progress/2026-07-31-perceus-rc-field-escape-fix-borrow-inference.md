- ✅ **Perceus RC field-escape fix (borrow inference)** — Fixes two bastion-surfaced bugs: (1) `local RC underflow at 0x... — aborting` crash in `FallbackController.call_json` and related patterns, (2) "second list-traversal returns None" on `get_assign`-style double reads of a `Conn`.  Root cause in `lib/tir/borrow.ml` `owned_in` (ECase arm): the scrutinee was classified as read-only when a `br_var` with a placeholder `TVar "_"` (always stamped by `Lower` on pattern-bound vars, regardless of the concrete constructor field type) escaped in an owning position.  Because `needs_rc(TVar "_") = false`, the escape was silently filtered out, so the caller never incref'd the parent — the extracted field was passed owned without rc transfer, and the next touch of the parent decref'd a cell whose rc was already 0.  Fix: a new `field_escape_owns` clause that inspects `br_vars` without gating on their placeholder type; follows through `let v = x in ...` alias chains to avoid over-promoting the common read-only `match conn do Conn(s) -> println(s) end` pattern.  Bastion test suite's `FallbackController.call_json` and `TypedMiddleware.full_pipeline` both green against the fixed compiler; the original bastion workaround (destructure-once) can be removed.
