Session.Theory

Session.Theory: the logic of March's protocols, and laws about it.

A protocol (protocol P do ... end) is a global type: who sends what to whom, where a role chooses, where it loops. Each role runs its projection, a local type. The compiler projects twice, in OCaml (the Chan(Role, Proto) typechecker and the @[endpoints] generator); this module is the specification both are validated against (march --check --guarantees, lemmas design §5.2, M6), and the laws below are proved once, here, for every protocol.

SessionRoles, labels and payload types are Int ids: the logic treats String contents as opaque, and the compiler maps names to ids when it emits a protocol as a SessionGlobal.

A loop is GLoop(body, after). The body's end is GVar(0), the loop's own back-edge (de Bruijn: the nearest enclosing loop); after is what a role that takes no part in the body does next. stop is GEnd. A choice's branches each carry the rest of the protocol (the steps after the choice are appended to every branch).
